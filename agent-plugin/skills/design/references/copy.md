# Interface copy

Words are part of the mockup: write the real ones at step 3, never placeholders.

- **The user's vocabulary**, not the system's: "notifications", not "webhook config"; the names the product already uses on other screens.
- **Buttons say what happens:** "Save changes", "Send invite", "Delete 3 campaigns", never "Submit", "OK" or "Yes".
- **One verb through a flow:** a button labelled Publish leads to "Published", not "Posted" or "Live".
- **Sentence case** for headings, buttons, labels and menus.
- **Errors say what happened and what to do,** in the user's terms, without apology or blame: "That email is already on the team. Invite someone else, or open their profile." Put the message next to the field it concerns.
- **Empty states say what goes here and offer the first action:** "No campaigns yet. Create one to start calling."
- **Loading says what is loading** when it takes more than a moment ("Loading 1,240 contacts…"); otherwise a skeleton in the content's shape.
- **Labels, not placeholders,** for form fields; placeholder text vanishes on input and fails contrast.
- **Numbers are formatted:** thousands separators, units, consistent decimals, and relative dates only where the exact one doesn't matter.
- **Destructive actions name the object and the count,** and say whether it can be undone.
- **No filler** in Operate mode: no welcome banners, taglines or "Here you can manage…" sentences. A heading and the content are enough.
